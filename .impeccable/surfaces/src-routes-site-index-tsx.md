# Home page (src/routes/_site/index.tsx)

Scope: the public home page and, by inheritance, the section, entry, and search pages. Visitor mode: Persuade (a search visitor must know what this is, trust it, and reach an entry in seconds).

Audience: Indian household, on a phone, mid-complaint or mid-cooking. Action: open an entry. Proof: the entries themselves and one accountable author. Constraints: existing name and heart-plus mark; light and dark themes; no fabricated claims; English only (the owner asked for no Hindi anywhere).

History: the roll (seed 52237cad, candidate 4, Indian medicine-carton label grammar) was built, reviewed (disposition fix), and then rejected by the owner in words ("I don't like the design, I like minimal stuff"). That is the standing exit taken by the user: convention, played straight, at full craft. The carton world is retired; nothing of it is preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet index. The page is a well-set list of entries under one honest sentence, and refuses hero blocks, cards, colour bands, and label strips.

OWN-WORLD: white ground (near-black in dark); Inter for everything; one accent, the mark's indigo, used for the mark, links on hover, and the primary button; hairline dividers and generous vertical space are the only structure; radius 8px on controls; no shadows, no gradients, no uppercase labels.

STORY: the visitor reads one sentence about what this is and who writes it, scans three short lists (Remedies, Tips, Recipes) with a one-line context under each title, and opens one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: slim header (mark + name, three section links, search field, theme toggle); a two-line headline sentence; a muted paragraph; then the first list begins under a hairline. Primary action is the first entry title.

FORM: minimal editorial index, the user-pinned direction; the seed's assignment no longer binds. Signature interaction: hover on a row tints it with the muted ground and underlines the title; nothing animates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
